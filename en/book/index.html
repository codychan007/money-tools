Many people do want to sort out their money, they just don't know where to start. What I do is simple: we lay the numbers out and look at them together. It's free; if you've done the check-up, bring your summary along.

How I work

Your money, your call. My job is to work out the numbers and explain them clearly.

When you talk to me, you won't get:

  • A product pitch the moment we sit down
  • Endless "so, have you decided?" follow-ups
  • Only the good news, or promises that sound too good to be true

If we finish and you don't need to do anything for now, that's a good outcome too.

What the chat looks like

  • Cost: Free
  • Length: About 30 minutes online; about 60 minutes in person
  • Format: Online (WhatsApp video / phone) or in person
  • Topics:
    • Understand where your savings and protection stand now
    • Work out concrete steps for the thing you most want to improve
    • Answer your questions on saving, protection or tax
