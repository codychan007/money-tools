How are MPF and take-home pay calculated?

Monthly-paid employees contribute {{MPF_RATE}} of their relevant income to MPF, and the employer contributes another {{MPF_RATE}}. Relevant income means basic pay plus commission, bonuses and so on, within these limits:

  • Earning under ${{MPF_MIN}} a month: you don't contribute, but your employer still pays {{MPF_RATE}}
  • Earning ${{MPF_MIN}} to ${{MPF_MAX}}: contribution = monthly income × {{MPF_RATE}}
  • Earning over ${{MPF_MAX}}: contributions are based on ${{MPF_MAX}}, so each side pays at most ${{MPF_CAP}} a month
