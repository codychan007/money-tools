    How are the five areas scored?

    Each area scores 0 to 100, and the overall score is the average of the five. The rules follow common rules of thumb and are not an official or industry standard:

    • Cash flow: full marks for saving 20% or more of your income each month
    • Emergency fund: full marks for cash covering 6 months of spending (including repayments)
    • Debt: full marks if repayments are 10% of income or less, 0 at 50% or more; carrying a credit card balance loses further points
    • Protection: having your own medical cover, and critical illness or life cover reviewed in the last two years
    • Money habits: saving or investing regularly beyond MPF, and tracking spending or keeping a budget
