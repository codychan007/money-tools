ABOUT ME

I'm Cody Chan, an insurance and financial planning consultant in Hong Kong.

I noticed that many people early in their careers are tired of money videos full of big talk, yet still don't have a clear idea of how much they can save each month or whether they are properly protected.

So I built this site: simple calculators and real, sourced numbers to help you work things out for yourself. If you'd like to talk it through afterwards, that's when you reach out to me.

How this site works

  • Every number has a source and a date: official sources such as the Government, HKMA, MPFA and Hospital Authority come first.
  • Updated regularly: markets daily, card offers re-checked on the 1st and 15th, healthcare policy monthly.
  • No product recommendations: general information and calculators only, and no card referral fees.
  • Your data stays on your device: calculations run in your browser and are never uploaded.
