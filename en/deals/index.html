Card rebates

Dining, shopping, getting around: which card pays you more?

Higher-rebate credit card offers, reviewed every two weeks, each with a link to the official terms. Expired offers disappear automatically.

Think before you swipe: high rebates usually come with minimum spending and caps, and spending more just to earn a rebate rarely pays. Foreign currency spending usually carries a separate transaction fee, so deduct it when working out the real rebate. Always pay your card in full each month; interest costs far more than any rebate.
Offer details are for reference only, compiled from issuers' public terms; the latest official terms prevail, and issuers may change or end offers at any time.
