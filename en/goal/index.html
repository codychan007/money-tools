How is it calculated?

The tool converts the yearly return into an equivalent monthly rate (monthly return = (1 + yearly return)1/12 − 1), assumes a fixed amount is added at the end of each month, and counts month by month until the total reaches your target. "How much a month?" works the other way round: the monthly amount needed to reach the target within your timeframe.

Example: $30,000 saved, $5,000 a month, target $500,000
